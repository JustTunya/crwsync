# Northstar Labs — Brand Identity & Visual Guidelines

**Version 2.1 • Author: Elena Rostova (Product Designer)**

---

## 1. Brand Essence

Northstar Labs represents precision, velocity, and clarity in collaborative engineering workflows. Our visual language is uncluttered, high-contrast, and performance-centric.

---

## 2. Logo & Iconography Usage

- **Symbol:** Four-point geometric star aligned with synchronized orbital nodes.
- **Minimum Clear Space:** 1.5x the height of the inner star mark.
- **Minimum Size:** 16px digital screen rendering / 0.25 inch print.

### Do's
- Use `northstar-logo-dark.svg` on light/neutral backgrounds.
- Use `northstar-logo-light.svg` on dark/terminal backgrounds.
- Maintain original aspect ratio without distortion.

### Don'ts
- Do not apply dropshadows or gradient fills to the vector mark.
- Do not rotate the star emblem off its 90-degree axis.

---

## 3. Color Palettes

### Primary Accent (`#3B82F6` — Electric Blue)
Used for active selection states, primary call-to-action buttons, and focus outlines.

### Neutral Scale (Zinc / Slate)
- **Canvas Dark:** `#0B0F19`
- **Surface Elevation 1:** `#111827`
- **Surface Elevation 2:** `#1F2937`
- **Border Default:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
